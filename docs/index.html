Buying guide选购指南

How to choose an Office AI copilot如何选择 Office AI Copilot

01

Start with the suite you already pay for先看你已有的套件

If your team lives in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, the built-in copilot is the lowest-friction option. It has the deepest context and the fewest integration surprises.如果团队日常使用 Microsoft 365 或 Google Workspace，内置 Copilot 是摩擦最小的选择，上下文最完整、集成问题最少。

02

Add-ins and agents fit existing workflows插件与代理融入现有流程

For one specific job — translating a contract, analyzing a workbook, building a deck — an add-in inside the app you already use beats switching suites. Compare the workflow first.当任务很具体——翻译合同、分析工作簿、生成演示——在现有应用内工作的插件通常比更换套件更合适。先比较工作流再决定。

03

Presentation tools replace the deck workflow演示工具替换做片流程

Gamma and its peers are not copilots for PowerPoint — they are a different way to make slides. Choose them when speed and iteration matter more than template compatibility.Gamma 等工具不是 PowerPoint 的 Copilot，而是制作幻灯片的另一种方式；当速度和迭代比模板兼容性更重要时选择它们。

04

PDF, data and meeting tools are complementsPDF、数据与会议工具是补充

PDF research, spreadsheet agents and meeting transcription serve adjacent jobs. They are usually worth adding only when that job is a daily one.PDF 研究、表格代理与会议转录服务于相邻任务，通常只在任务高频时值得额外引入。

What is an Office AI copilot?什么是 Office AI Copilot？

An AI assistant that works with office software such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Docs, Sheets, Slides, email or meetings. Some are built into a suite, some are add-ins or agents, and some replace part of the workflow entirely.与 Word、Excel、PowerPoint、Docs、Sheets、Slides、邮件或会议等办公软件配合的 AI 助手：有的内置于套件，有的是插件或代理，有的则整体替换部分工作流程。

Which Office AI copilot works with Microsoft 365?哪些 Office AI Copilot 支持 Microsoft 365？

Microsoft 365 Copilot is the native option for Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams. Add-ins such as Claude for Excel, Claude for PowerPoint and TopCopilot also work inside Microsoft Office.
