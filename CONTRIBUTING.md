# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this directory useful.

## What belongs here

Submit products with a clear office-work use case: documents, spreadsheets, presentations, PDF, email, meetings, workplace search, or knowledge management. Global products and English-language resources are welcome; Chinese-language products are welcome too.

## Requirements

1. Link to the official product page, not a directory, affiliate page, or review.
2. Describe a concrete workflow in one or two sentences.
3. Do not claim a feature is available to every user unless the official source says so.
4. Mention important constraints when known: plan, region, platform, beta status, or add-in requirement.
5. Keep names, categories, and tags consistent with `data/products.json`.

Promotional submissions, duplicate entries, misleading descriptions, and unrelated products will be declined.
