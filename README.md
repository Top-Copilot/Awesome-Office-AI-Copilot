## Why this list exists

Office AI is no longer one product category. Some copilots live inside an existing suite, some operate as add-ins, and others replace part of the office workflow entirely. This list focuses on products with a clear document, spreadsheet, presentation, PDF, meeting, or workplace-knowledge use case.

- Official product links first; no affiliate links.
- Global coverage: English-language and international products are welcome.
- Product status and capabilities are checked against public information; availability can vary by plan, region, and account type.
- Last reviewed: **2026-07-31**.
